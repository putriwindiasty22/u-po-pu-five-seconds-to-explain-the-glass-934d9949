The one sentence a house cat would say in its own defence in the five seconds
after knocking a glass off the table:

> It was squatting in my sunbeam without paying rent, so I evicted it — you can thank me later, preferably in treats.

`main.ts` writes that sentence to `/output/entry.txt` (or `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` outside the grader).
